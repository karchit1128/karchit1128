## Building at the intersection of silicon and software

I'm Archit, a 2nd year ECE student at BMSIT, Bengaluru. Electronics has always felt like magic to me — a few layers of silicon, and suddenly it can think, sense, and talk to the world.

I don't just study this stuff, I build with it. Over the last year I've worked across robotics, AI tools, and embedded systems — not because I couldn't pick a lane, but because I genuinely wanted to try it all.
